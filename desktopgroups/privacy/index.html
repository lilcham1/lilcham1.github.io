DesktopGroups

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

DesktopGroups works entirely on your PC. It has no accounts, analytics or ads, makes no internet connections, and sends no data to me or anyone else.

What it stores, on your PC

  • Items you put in a group: %AppData%\DesktopGroups\<group>\
  • Group styles and settings: %AppData%\DesktopGroups\groups.json
  • Generated tile icons: %LocalAppData%\DesktopGroups\Icons

Removing it

Delete your groups first so their items go back to the desktop, quit the app, then delete the folders above.
