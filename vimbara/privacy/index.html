Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Vimbara is a personal project run by one person, not a company. This page explains what it stores, who processes it, and how to get it removed. The same summary is in the app at vimbara.vercel.app/privacy.

What is stored

Your account email (and name or photo if you sign in with Google). Everything you enter: tasks, events, work shifts and imported schedules, reminders, work logs, income and expenses, repeats, your monthly plan, savings goals, workouts and workout plans, your body weights and progress photos, your profile (first name, jobs, positions and pay, monthly income) and settings such as currency and time zone. If you turn on notifications, the push address your browser gives for that device.

Each account can only see its own data. As the person running the app, I can technically see the database.

AI features

The daily summary, the savings tips, the goal planner and the “Ask Vimbara” assistant use Google Gemini on its free tier. When you ask the assistant something, the app sends your question and a summary of your own data to answer it: today's shifts, events, tasks and reminders, hours worked per job, this month's plan totals and repeats, your goals, your workout plans and recent workouts, your body-weight trend, your category names and your first name. Pay rates, notes on transactions and your account details are never sent. Google may use free-tier content to improve its products.

Progress photos

Progress photos are shrunk on your phone before they're uploaded, which also removes location data. They're stored in a private folder that only your account can open, shown through links that expire after a short time, and never sent to the AI. Deleting your account deletes them too.

Your calendar link

The calendar link in Me → Calendar link is private. Anyone who has the link can see the shifts and events in it, so only share it with calendars you use.

Selling and sharing

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

Getting your data, or deleting it

In Me → Backup you can download everything you entered, or your transactions as a CSV. In Me → Account you can delete your account, which permanently removes all your data.

This website

lilxcham.com counts visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you across other sites. It only records which pages are viewed, where a visit came from (for example a search engine), and the country, browser and type of device. Only I can see these numbers.
