01

Your day, your money and your workouts

Today

One timeline for today and tomorrow: shifts, events, reminders, bills and tasks due, with an AI summary of your day.

Ask Vimbara

Ask “How many hours this month?” or “What can I spend today?” on any screen. It answers from your own data, and anything it adds waits as a card until you confirm it.

Shifts from your roster

Paste each job's roster Google Sheets link once. It's read again every night, with each month's shifts, hours and pay, and your own changes kept.

Tasks

Due dates, priorities and repeats: daily, weekly or monthly. Tick one off and it moves to its next date.

Reminders

One-off or repeating push notifications, and an optional reminder before each shift.

Money and a monthly plan

Income and spending by category, and a plan for the month: your income minus repeats, goals and savings, so you see what you can still spend this month and per day.

Gym

Copy a starter plan or build your own, then track each set live with a rest timer and plate calculator. 880 exercises with photos and steps, demo videos for common lifts, and progress charts with your records.

Bank import

Upload a .csv or .xlsx statement. Categories are guessed from your past entries, and duplicates are skipped.

Savings goals

Progress, a suggested monthly amount and a live finish date. Plan a goal with AI; the maths is done by the app.

02

Yours, and only yours

Each account only sees its own data. You can download everything you've entered as one file, export your transactions as a CSV, or delete your account and all its data from Me → Account. Ask Vimbara and the other AI features send your question and a summary of your own data to Google Gemini; pay rates, transaction notes and account details are never sent. Read the full privacy page.

Questions

Anything else? Email lilxcham@proton.me.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. For reminders on iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).

Does the AI change things on its own?

No. Ask Vimbara prepares changes as cards, and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Can I track my workouts?

Yes. Gym has plans, a live tracker for each set, an exercise library with demos, and your history and records.

Can I bring in my bank history?

Yes. Under Money → Import, upload a .csv or .xlsx statement, check the columns and pick the rows to add.
