Your day and your money

Today

One timeline for today and tomorrow: shifts, events, reminders, bills and tasks due, with an AI summary of your day.

Just tell it

Type “lunch 12.50” or “dentist Fri 3pm”. It drafts the entry, and nothing is saved until you press Add.

Shifts from your roster

Paste your roster's Google Sheets link once. See each month's shifts, hours and pay, and keep your own changes on every refresh.

Tasks

Due dates, priorities and repeats: daily, weekly or monthly. Tick one off and it moves to its next date.

Reminders

One-off or repeating push notifications, and an optional reminder before each shift.

Money and budgets

Income and spending by category, monthly budgets, and a heads-up when a category reaches 80% or 100%.

Bank import

Upload a .csv or .xlsx statement. Categories are guessed from your past entries, and duplicates are skipped.

Savings goals

Progress, a suggested monthly amount and a live finish date. Plan a goal with AI; the maths is done by the app.

Example goal: 64% saved

Calendar link

A private link that shows your shifts and events in Apple, Google or Outlook calendar.

Yours, and only yours

Each account only sees its own data. You can download everything you've entered as one file, export your transactions as a CSV, or delete your account and all its data from Settings. AI features send a summary of your day and your spending by category to Google Gemini, never account numbers. Read the full privacy page.

Questions

Anything else? Email lilxcham@proton.me.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. For reminders on iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).

Does the AI change things on its own?

No. It drafts suggestions, and nothing is saved until you press Add.

Can I bring in my bank history?

Yes. Under Money → Import, upload a .csv or .xlsx statement, check the columns and pick the rows to add.
