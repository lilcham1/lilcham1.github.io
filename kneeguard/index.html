01

What it catches

Malware links

Known malware addresses from threat feeds, and direct downloads of programs and scripts.

Phishing and scams

Several scam feeds, fake brand names like “dlscord”, lookalike letters, giveaway and login bait.

Known scam images

Block an image once and resized or re-compressed copies are caught after that.

Scam messages

Pitches with no link at all, like “giveaway, DM me” or requests for seed phrases.

NSFW links and invites

Adult sites, and invites to servers Discord marks as age-restricted.

Link spam

The same link across channels, floods of links, and mass mentions.

Discord invites

Invites and invite redirectors. Invites to your own server are allowed.

Money links and walletsOptional

Donation and payment links to someone's page, and real crypto wallet addresses (checksum-checked), if you want them blocked.

Self-promotionOptional

Referral and affiliate links, “use my code” messages and links to someone's channel. Your server's own sponsor codes stay allowed.

02

Fair to your members

  • QuietNo public warnings or bot chatter. The message is deleted, the sender gets a private DM, and the full details go to your log channel.
  • Guesses wait for a moderatorWhen it's only fairly sure, the message is deleted but nobody is muted, kicked or banned until a moderator clicks Confirm.
  • Your rulesDelete only, warn, timeout, kick, softban or ban, set per protection for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd offence, or all at once with the Relaxed, Standard or Strict preset. Bans and kicks can also clean up what the sender just posted.
  • Hacked-account modeWhen a long-time member suddenly posts a confirmed scam, they're muted for a day and told how to secure their account, instead of being kicked or banned.
  • AppealsTimeout and ban messages can carry an Appeal button. Appeals land in your log channel with Lift and Reject, one per punishment.
  • Raid and impersonation alertsA heads-up in your log when lots of new accounts join at once, or someone copies a staff name, with Kick, Ban and Ignore buttons.
  • Weekly reportOnce a week, a summary in your log channel of what was caught, what was punished and what still waits for a review. A quiet week posts nothing.
03

Light on data

Messages are checked in memory and not saved. The only exception is a message your moderators confirm as a scam: its words are kept, without their order, so reworded reposts get caught. Server settings are encrypted, offence records are deleted after your offence window (24 hours by default), and everything stored for a server is deleted when KneeGuard is removed, apart from its subscription record (the server ID, its trial and paid-until dates and payment references), so its trial and subscription carry over if it's added back.

Questions

Anything else? Ask in the KneeGuard support server or email lilxchamoun@gmail.com. News and new scams KneeGuard blocks: @Kneeguardbot on X.

How do I set it up?

Add it to your server; your 30-day trial starts straight away. Run /setup (it needs Manage Server), pick a log channel and how each kind of link is handled. Drag KneeGuard's role above the members it should act on, since Discord ignores actions on anyone higher.

What happens after the 30 days?

Three days before the end, KneeGuard posts a reminder in your log channel (or a staff channel) with what it caught so far. When the trial ends it switches off and stays in your server, idle, until someone subscribes. It never leaves on its own. Each server gets one trial.

How do I pay?

Press Subscribe with crypto on the bot's message (you need Manage Server), pick Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Litecoin or USDC, and send the exact amount to the address shown. Only you see it. Once the payment is confirmed, usually within minutes, KneeGuard switches on for 30 days and says so in your server. Payments are handled by NOWPayments.

How do I cancel? Can I get a refund?

Each payment covers 30 days and nothing renews by itself, so to cancel you simply don't pay again; KneeGuard switches off when the month ends. Crypto payments are final, so there are no refunds. That's what the free trial is for.

Will my price go up?

Not for early supporters: the first 50 servers keep $10/month for as long as they stay subscribed. Prices for new subscribers may change later.

Will it ban people by mistake?

When it's only guessing, it deletes the message and waits for a moderator to confirm before anyone is punished. You can turn that off if you want instant action.

Does it post warnings in my channels?

No. Senders get a private DM (you can turn that off), and the details go to your log channel.

Can I allow some links?

Yes, there's a whitelist page in /setup.

Does it store our messages?

No. Messages are checked in memory and thrown away. If your moderators confirm a message as a scam, its words are kept (without their order) to catch reposts. See the privacy policy.
